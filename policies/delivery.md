# Delivery policy

Standard parcel delivery arrives in 3 to 7 business days and is free on orders over $99. White glove delivery for furniture is scheduled within 2 to 4 weeks, and our delivery partner calls 48 hours ahead to confirm a 4-hour window. White glove includes in-room placement, assembly and packaging removal for a flat $199. You can reschedule or change a white glove delivery date at no charge up to 72 hours before the appointment, from the Orders page or by contacting Client Care.
