# Returns policy

Most items can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Sofas, sectionals, upholstered furniture and mattresses have a 30-day return window and a $149 return pickup fee. Custom and made-to-order items, such as custom fabric or monogrammed linens, are final sale. Items must be unused and in original condition, and returns without a receipt receive store credit. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.
